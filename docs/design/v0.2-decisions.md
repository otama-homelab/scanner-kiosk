# v0.2 adoption decisions — DEC-01

**Recorded:** 2026-11-10. This is the single canonical DEC-01 register. The [client/monetization comparison](client-monetization.md) supplies CLIENT-01 / APP-01 evidence and dated official conditions; it is not another adoption registry.

**Claim classes:** VERIFIED = existing source/test/measurement within its actual scope; CITED = official platform/policy sources retrieved2026-11-10; ASSUMED = proposed benefit, demand, willingness to pay, device compatibility/performance, policy durability or cost not directly observed. Recommendations and backlog triggers do not authorize implementation, purchases, private sends or live operations. Local `.planning/` evidence links are gitignored references, not claims of public accessibility. No raw documents, credentials, account identifiers or private paths are reproduced.
