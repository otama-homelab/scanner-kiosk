# Client and monetization comparison

**Evidence reviewed:** 2026-11-10. Requirements: CLIENT-01 / APP-01. This is comparison evidence for the [canonical DEC-01 record](v0.2-decisions.md); it makes no client or commercial adoption decision.

## Scope and evidence classes

- **VERIFIED:** existing source or Phase 44–49 observations, within the cited test/fixture/runtime scope. This phase ran no product benchmark or device test.
- **CITED:** official platform/policy documentation retrieved during [Phase 50 research](../../.planning/phases/50-client-monetization/50-RESEARCH.md) on **2026-11-10**. Availability, eligibility and mutable terms require confirmation for an actual release or sale; these are not device measurements.
- **ASSUMED:** proposed subsets, demand, willingness to pay, compatibility/performance, policy durability and cost scenarios without direct observations. A possible benefit is not measured demand.

The current baseline is a household server application. Existing public source does not establish a public hosted product, tenant isolation or commercial readiness. No prototype, SDK, dependency, account, device, billing, deployment, push or private-document transmission is part of this comparison. Relative `.planning/` links identify local evidence, which is gitignored; they do not promise publicly accessible reports. Public conclusions below contain no raw documents, credentials, private origins or private filesystem paths.

## Client capability comparison

The true browser-complete definition is processing and document storage without a remote document-processing server. A static package or Service Worker alone does not meet it. The proposed maintainable subset is **photo/file input → local preview/manual crop/rotate → explicit export**, with an origin-local catalog for reopen/delete/capacity reporting. Every proposed capability remains **ASSUMED, unimplemented and unmeasured**.

## Demand and cost assumptions

**ASSUMED value hypotheses:** household capture→save convenience; local-only privacy; recurring OCR/document management. No interviews, survey, purchase/renewal experiment, public customer demand or willingness-to-pay evidence exists. Official APIs, competitor prices and one household's use do not supply it. Future evidence must establish recent actual workflows/problems, an unmet benefit beyond the current Web app, local/cloud preference and purchase/continuation behavior at a concrete offer. Sample size, price and conversion thresholds remain unset.

**VERIFIED resource observations:** [OCR synthetic comparison](japanese-ocr-comparison.md) has64 actual runs: single-page warm p50 Tesseract974–1171ms / NDLOCR7280–7402ms; maximum warm peak RSS195.3 /622.0MiB. Two warm processes per pair are observations, not population p95, resident-model speed, phone performance, cloud price or sustained capacity. [Docling](structure-comparison.md) has **zero candidate rows**:900s/2048MiB RSS/1024MiB temporary-disk ceilings are intended limits, not measurements.

**Illustrative arithmetic only; every input is ASSUMED, not forecast or configured retention:**30documents/month/household,100active public tenants,3pages/document,2MiB/page, original+derived factor3, average retention30days. Steady-state storage = active tenants × documents/month × pages/document × MiB/page × factor × retention-days/30:540MiB household, approximately52.7GiB public, excluding backup/replica/temp. Vary tenants, frequency, pages, bytes, derived multiplier and retention independently;30days does not change current TTL.

Monthly cost = fixed hosting + stored GiB × storage rate + egress GiB × egress rate + processing jobs × cost/job + backup + support hours × hourly cost + allocated build/device/account cost. Electricity, storage/egress rates, workload, capacity and maintenance/support time are unmeasured. Household incremental and allocated costs both matter; existing NAS/power is not free. Public free tenants also consume resources.

Contribution/payer = price − store/PSP charges − variable processing/storage/egress/support/refund cost. Break-even payers = fixed cost / contribution **only when contribution >0**; active tenants and payers differ. Paid downloads require a separate lifetime support/update calculation. Regional store commission plus alternative PSP cost must both be included. No positive margin, configured budget, SLA or revenue forecast follows from these formulas.

## Conditions for a later experiment

**ASSUMED evidence gates, not operational authorization:** browser-local work needs a concrete unmet local/offline need, a fixed browser/OS matrix and agreed retention/export/reopen/multiple-tab/quota/eviction/crash/data-loss behavior before a bounded input→review→export measurement. Native work additionally needs observed value beyond Web, supported camera/storage/restart behavior and maintenance capacity. Commercial work needs purchase/continuation evidence and positive unit contribution, then selected geography/distribution/account/program/PSP and refreshed applicable official terms. No such experiment or sale is authorized by this comparison.
